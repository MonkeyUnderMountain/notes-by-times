\subsection[status=draft,tag=0000000,label=\labelNFM{subsec:berk}]{Berkovich analytification}

Let $X$ be a connected smooth variety over a non-archimedean complete algebraically closed  field $\kkk$.
Then we define
\[ X^\an \coloneqq \left\{ (y,|\cdot| ) \;\middle|\; y \in X \text{ scheme point }, |\cdot|: \rkk(y) \to \bbR \text{ multiplicative seimnorm extending } |\cdot| \text{ on } \kkk   \right\} \]
The topology is given by the weak topology given by the functions $\wp: X^\an \to X, (y,|\cdot|) \mapsto y$ and $f: \wp^{-1}(y) \to \bbR$ with $U \subset X$ open and $f \in \calO_X(U)$, $y \in U$.

We have some properties.
\begin{enumerate}
	\item $X \mapsto X^\an$ is functorial.
	\item separated $X$ gives Hausdorff $X^\an$;
	\item proper gives compact,
	\item connected gives path connected.
	\item Krull dim gives the topological dimension.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{theorem}[label=\labelNFM{thm:thm1}, status=draft, tag=0000000]
	$X^\an$ always strongly deformation restricts onto a ``finite polyhedral complex".
	The finite polyhedral complex is related to the ``tropical space''.
\end{theorem}
