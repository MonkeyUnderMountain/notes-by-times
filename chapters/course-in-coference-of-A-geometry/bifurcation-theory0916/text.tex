\subsection[status=draft,tag=0000000,label=\labelNFM{subsec:ari}]{Arithmetic original}

\begin{theorem}[label=\labelNFM{thm:lang}, status=draft, tag=0000000]
	Let $C \subset \bbC^2$ be an irreducible curve, TFAE
	\begin{enumerate}
		\item $|C \cap \mu_\infty \times \mu_\infty| = \infty$;
		\item $C$ is a component of $x^n - \xi y^m = 0$ with $\xi \in \mu_\infty$.
	\end{enumerate}
\end{theorem}

The $\mu_\infty \times \mu_\infty$ are special points in $(\bbC^*)^2$.
And the curves in (b) are special curves (translation of algebraic subgroup of $(\bbC^*)^2$).
